# References

This directory contains verified bibliographic material and reading notes that support public research. References are added only after source verification. The following foundational validity sources are cited in the research notes and are formatted in APA 7th edition style.

American Educational Research Association, American Psychological Association, & National Council on Measurement in Education. (2014). *Standards for educational and psychological testing*. American Educational Research Association. https://ncme.org/resources/books/testing-standards/

Kane, M. T. (2013). Validating the interpretations and uses of test scores. *Journal of Educational Measurement, 50*(1), 1–73. https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.12000

Messick, S. (1989). Validity. In R. L. Linn (Ed.), *Educational measurement* (3rd ed., pp. 13–103). American Council on Education/Macmillan Publishing Company. https://www.ets.org/research/policy_research_reports/publications/chapter/1989/hwrq.html

These sources provide established measurement-validity foundations. The Score → Evidence → Claim framing and the claim–evidence matrix are Psychmetrica Lab's proposed applications of those foundations to AI evaluation; they have not been empirically validated.
